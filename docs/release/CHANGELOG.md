# EZRASCORES Changelog
All notable user-facing changes are documented here.

This follows a Keep-a-Changelog style with product-friendly language.

## [Unreleased]
### Added
- A new look: My Team, Matches, Play, Leagues and Me tabs, with clearer text and a much slimmer header on phones.
- Pick your team when you first open the app, then a quick how-to-play.
- Today card on My Team: your daily quests in one checklist with a button for each.
- Higher or Lower is back: guess which team has more league points, and beat your best score.
- Goal, kick-off and full-time alerts for your team, even when the app is closed (turn on in Settings).
- A full-time result card for your team's match, showing how your prediction did, plus last week's league champion on the Leagues tab.
- Matches grouped by competition with your team first, and each match opens in its own panel.
- Faster loading on phones and works offline with the last scores you saw.

### Improved
- You can pinch to zoom again, secondary text is easier to read, and animations calm down when your device asks for reduced motion.
- Match stats use plain names ("Expected goals (xG)", "Shots inside box"), and older notifications show their day or date.
- The World Cup is tucked away until the next tournament.

### Fixed
- Matches no longer stay stuck on "live" long after the final whistle.
- "Explore A Random Player" now picks someone from your own team's squad, so you can actually find them, and its celebration shows the right points.
- Finishing a quest while signed out no longer promises points you don't get; it asks you to sign in to bank them.
- The "Predict Today's Feature Match" quest no longer gets stuck on "Loading" when you're signed out — it now asks you to sign in, and offers a retry if loading fails.

## [2026-02-27]
### Added
- League invite flows with shareable links and deferred join after login.
- Account recovery improvements with clearer reset UX and messaging.

### Improved
- Fun zone interaction flow and quest continuity.
- Navigation and section-level loading handling.

### Fixed
- Multiple reliability issues across fixtures/team interactions and account flows.
